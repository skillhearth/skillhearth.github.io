How it works

  1. Download a skill (a folder with a SKILL.md file and a few reference files).
  2. Unzip it and put the folder where your AI agent reads skills (its skills folder), or paste its contents into a chat.
  3. Give it your real information. It works from that and asks about gaps.

For the free skill, this command was observed working in our own test:

npx skills add skillhearth/resume-bullet-rewriter

Observed in our own test only; we don't know how it behaves in your setup.

Free: resume-bullet-rewriter

Free · MIT license

Paste your resume bullets and get each one rewritten side by side (before/after). It flags vague, repeated and weak-verb bullets and uses only facts you provide. Where a fact is missing it inserts an [ADD] marker and asks one short question. It never invents employers, numbers, tools or results, and it makes no promises about interviews or jobs.

debt-payoff-budget-planner

US$9
Educational use only. Not financial, tax or legal advice. Simplified interest model (annual rate ÷ 12, no fees). It recommends no financial product or provider.

Enter your income, expenses and debts. It computes your monthly leftover and compares avalanche and snowball payoff, showing the arithmetic, using only your numbers.

  • Shows its assumptions: fixed rates, annual rate ÷ 12 compounded monthly, no fees, constant income and expenses. Real terms can differ.
  • Won't guess rates or fees you didn't give it, and won't say which method you should pick.
  • If your budget has no leftover, it shows the shortfall instead of a misleading projection.

Personal-use license, educational only.

Honest limits and how we tested

  • Tested on: our own fictional test cases (three per skill), not on any specific platform. Each skill was run by the same AI that wrote it, which is a weak test. We don't claim compatibility with any platform; the skills follow the SKILL.md folder format.
  • The debt planner's arithmetic was checked against separate code, on fictional numbers.
  • No outcome promises: no interviews, no jobs, no replies, no financial results.
  • The skills work only from what you give them. They don't verify your claims and don't state country-specific rules.
  • The scam-signal list in job-application-tailor is not exhaustive. Always verify an employer yourself.
  • No reviews, user counts or ratings are shown because we have none to show.

FAQ

What is a SKILL.md?
A plain-text instruction file an AI assistant can follow. The skill itself is text; it runs no code on your computer.

Does it guarantee anything?
No. No interviews, no jobs, no financial outcomes.

Is my data sent to you?
This page has no scripts, trackers or forms. What you type into your AI assistant goes to that assistant, under its own terms, not to us.
